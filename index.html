Research Focus

The trajectory from my Master's work to my current PhD research.

My M.Sc. research focused on deep-learning-based classification of Wireless Capsule Endoscopy images — EfficientNet architectures, data augmentation, ensembling, and hyperparameter tuning, reaching 95.75% accuracy. That work surfaced an observation that shapes my PhD direction: strong overall accuracy can still hide substantial differences in how well a model performs on individual classes.

My PhD research question: how can CNN-based models be structured to achieve more consistent performance across classes while maintaining trustworthy, reliable predictions — especially under limited-data conditions?

I'm investigating this through model and representation structure — attention mechanisms and learned representations as intervention points — with information-theoretic principles (entropy and related measures) as one candidate mechanism. The entropy-regularized channel-attention work below is an initial investigation of that direction, not the whole of it — the broader question stays open to other architectural and uncertainty-aware approaches as the research develops.
